# Content refinement example

## Input

> We have operationalized the remediation workflow and need stakeholders to
> action exceptions by Friday.

## Refined output

Review and resolve your assigned exceptions by Friday. Use the remediation
workflow to record the decision and supporting evidence.

## Why it changed

- Leads with the reader's action.
- Replaces “operationalized” and “action” with direct verbs.
- Preserves the Friday deadline without inventing a date.
- Does not claim that the workflow or evidence has been confirmed.

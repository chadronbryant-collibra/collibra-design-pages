exercise inputs, not a Collibra procedure.

## Source supplied by the author

## Reader draft

# Prepare a team workshop

Prepare an agenda and meeting space so the team can start the workshop.

## Before you start

Ask the organizer for the topics and intended meeting date.

## Steps

1. Collect the workshop topics from the organizer.
2. Check room availability for the intended date. If no room is available, ask
   the organizer to choose an online meeting or another date.
3. Confirm the meeting format with the organizer.
4. Send the agreed agenda to the participants.
5. For an in-person workshop, check the room setup before the workshop.

## Check the result

The participants have the agenda and the organizer has confirmed the meeting
format. For an in-person workshop, the room is ready for the session.

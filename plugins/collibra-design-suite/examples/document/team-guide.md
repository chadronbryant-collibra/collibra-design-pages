# Resolve a data-definition question

Status: synthetic example · proposed content

## What this guide helps you do

Use this guide when two teams use different definitions for the same business
term and a shared decision is needed.

## Before you start

- Name the reader who will make or confirm the decision.
- Link only evidence you are authorized to share.
- Mark unconfirmed statements as proposed.

## Make the decision

1. Write the reader's question in one sentence.
2. Show the two definitions without changing their meaning.
3. Explain the practical difference in plain language.
4. Name the decision owner and confirmation date.

## Example decision record

| Field | Synthetic example |
| --- | --- |
| Term | Active customer |

## Check before sharing

Confirm that the title states the task, the decision is visible before the
detail, links have meaningful labels, tables have headers, and color is not the
only way status is communicated.

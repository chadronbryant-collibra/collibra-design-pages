# Decision-flow slide outline

Status: synthetic example · proposed structure

Reader question: Where does a proposed definition become an approved one?

1. **Propose** — A contributor supplies a definition and source.
2. **Review** — A steward checks meaning, evidence, and affected users.
3. **Decide** — The accountable owner approves, returns, or rejects it.
4. **Publish** — The confirmed definition is made available with its owner and date.

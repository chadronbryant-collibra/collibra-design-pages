Prepare a team workshop

For the coordinator arranging a workshop and the organizer choosing its format.

When to use this SOP

Use this process when an organizer asks you to arrange a team workshop. It covers the agenda, meeting format and room readiness.

Before you start

  • Get the proposed date, topics and expected number of participants from the organizer.
  • Confirm whether anyone will join remotely or needs a particular room setup.

Who does what

RoleResponsibility
OrganizerConfirms the topics, meeting format and any date change.
CoordinatorChecks availability, prepares the agenda and checks the room.

Arrange the workshop

  1. Collect the topics and intended date from the organizer.
  2. Check whether a suitable room is available. If it is unavailable, ask the organizer to choose an online meeting or another date.
  3. Confirm the format and date with the organizer before preparing the final agenda.
  4. Send the agreed agenda to the participants through the team's usual invitation process.
  5. For an in-person or hybrid meeting, complete the room-readiness check. Skip that check for an online-only meeting.

Check the result

The organizer has confirmed the format and date. Participants have the agreed agenda. For an in-person or hybrid workshop, the room is ready.

If plans change

Ask the organizer to confirm the new arrangement. Update the invitation and agenda together so participants receive one consistent set of instructions.

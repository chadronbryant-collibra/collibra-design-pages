QUICK TASK · EXAMPLE DATA

Check a workshop room

For the coordinator preparing an in-person or hybrid workshop.

Check that the room can support the agreed workshop before participants arrive.

Before you start

Confirm the date, format and participant needs with the organizer. This task accompanies Prepare a team workshop and applies only to in-person or hybrid meetings.

Check the room

  1. Confirm that the booked room matches the invitation and has enough places for the expected participants.
  2. Arrange the furniture and materials for the agreed activities.
  3. For a hybrid workshop, check that a remote participant can hear the room and see the shared content.
  4. If something essential is unavailable, tell the organizer before the session. Agree an alternative rather than assuming a replacement will work.

Check the result

The space, materials and joining setup support the agreed workshop. The organizer knows about any remaining limitation.

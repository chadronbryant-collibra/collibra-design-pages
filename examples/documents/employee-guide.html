Join a learning session

For a colleague registering for a session in an example learning portal.

Find a session that suits your learning goal and confirm your place before adding it to your calendar.

Before you start

  • Have access to your learning portal.
  • Check the session's time zone and any preparation listed by the organizer.

Register for a session

  1. Open the learning portal and choose Browse sessions.
  2. Find the session you want. Open its details and check the date, time zone and format.
  3. If the session has places available, choose Register. If it is full, choose another session or join the waiting list when that option is offered.
  4. Look for the registration confirmation. Once your place is confirmed, add the session to your calendar.

Check the result

The session appears under My learning with a confirmed place. Your calendar shows the correct time zone and joining details.

If you cannot attend

Open the session under My learning and use the cancellation option. If you cannot find it, contact the session organizer using the details on the session page.
